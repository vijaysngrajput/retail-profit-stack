What you learned

Five more things an AI data engineer knows.

  1. Decide what correct means before a model writes code.You worked out small test cases by hand. They turned every draft, from a person or a model, from something you must read into something you can check.
  2. Keep the rule separate from the engine.A rule written as a contract ran on pandas and on SQL with identical results, and is ready for PySpark. The business rule outlives the technology.
  3. Sum first, then join.Joining order lines straight to refunds counted a line twice and raised no error. Six small transformations are easier to get right, and far easier to check, than one large one.
  4. Choose a model on evidence, and read your prompt first.The same tasks and tests gave each model three numbers: accuracy, cost, and speed. And fixing one misleading sentence in the prompt lifted three models by more than changing model would have.
  5. Tests gate. People review.Given the tests as a tool, a model repaired a broken draft by itself. But a loop only helps a model that can act on a failure, and passing the tests makes code a candidate, not a release.
